About

Who I am

I’m Pascal Nague, a Computer Science student at RPTU Kaiserslautern with a strong focus on Machine Learning, Data and AI systems.

I enjoy understanding how intelligent systems work — from the data they consume to the way their performance, reliability and efficiency are measured.

My work combines Python, PyTorch, data analysis, machine learning experimentation and performance benchmarking. My bachelor project focuses on efficient data loading for PyTorch, particularly the pipeline between storage, preprocessing and model training.

I am especially interested in building practical systems that are measurable, understandable and useful in real-world environments.
