About

Who i am

I’m Pascal, a Computer Science student at RPTU Kaiserslautern specializing in Machine Learning. I’m curious about how intelligent systems work, from the data they learn from to the way their performance is measured. I enjoy making complex ideas easier to understand and connecting what I learn with practical challenges.

I work with Python, PyTorch, and data analysis tools to explore datasets, build machine learning prototypes, and evaluate their results. My bachelor’s project focuses on efficient data loading for PyTorch, giving me hands-on experience with the pipeline between storage and model training. Through my projects, I aim to build solutions that are useful, understandable, and grounded in measurable results.

Services

I help businesses and teams make sense of their data, automate repetitive tasks, and evaluate AI solutions. From data preparation to machine learning prototypes, I focus on practical results, clear insights, and solutions tailored to your needs.

Data Cleaning and Preparation

Clean CSV or Excel files with duplicates, missing values, and inconsistent formats addressed, alongside a reusable script and a data quality report.

Exploratory Data Analysis

An analysis of sales, customer, or operational data, supported by visualizations and clear findings.

Simple Interactive Dashboards

An interactive dashboard to track key metrics, filter data, and simplify reporting.

Data Workflow Automation

Scripts that merge files, transform exports, calculate metrics, or generate reports automatically.

Machine Learning Prototyping

An initial classification, regression, or clustering model, compared against a simple baseline and delivered with a documented evaluation.

ML Model Evaluation

A performance assessment covering precision, recall, F1 score, confusion matrices, error categories, and improvement opportunities.

Chatbot and LLM Assistant Quality Testing

A test dataset, an evaluation rubric, and a report covering incorrect responses, ambiguous requests, and edge cases.

French Transcription Quality Assessment

Audio-to-transcript checks, error classification, and transcription error measurements when reliable reference transcripts are available.

PyTorch Data Pipeline Performance Analysis

Throughput and loading-time measurements, DataLoader configuration comparisons, and evidence-based recommendations.

Technical Training and Tutoring

Hands-on sessions, annotated notebooks, or introductory workshops on Python, data analysis, and ML fundamentals.
